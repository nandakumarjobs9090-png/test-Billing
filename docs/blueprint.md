# **App Name**: Chai Ledger

## Core Features:

- Product Catalog Management: Shop owners can easily add, edit, or remove tea items and snacks, including their prices and stock levels.
- New Sale Transaction: An intuitive interface to quickly select items, specify quantities, calculate the total, apply discounts, and generate a new bill for a customer.
- Bill Summary & Finalization: Display a clear, itemized summary of the current bill before finalization, with an option to mark it as paid or print it.
- Transaction History View: Browse a comprehensive list of all past sales transactions, allowing filtering by date or payment status to track sales performance.
- AI-powered Menu Description Tool: A generative AI tool that assists shop owners in creating compelling and aromatic descriptions for new tea blends or food items based on basic ingredients or desired flavors.

## Style Guidelines:

- Color scheme: Light mode. The palette evokes warmth, comfort, and the natural richness of tea. Primary color: a deep, inviting terracotta (#A34E3F). This warm red-brown signifies stability and the earthy tones of a traditional tea shop. Background color: A soft, creamy off-white (#FCF8F5) providing a clean, airy feel that allows the content to stand out. Accent color: A rich, deep wine red (#731F2E) for highlights and interactive elements, adding a touch of elegance and visual interest.
- Headline font: 'Alegreya' (humanist serif) for an elegant, intellectual feel, perfect for product names and titles. Body font: 'PT Sans' (humanist sans-serif) for clear, readable body text, suitable for billing details and item descriptions, offering a modern yet approachable look.
- Utilize simple, clean line icons related to tea, food, billing, and transactions (e.g., a teacup, a food item, a cash register, a receipt icon). Icons should be subtle yet clearly identifiable, enhancing navigation and user experience without clutter.
- A clean, card-based layout for product listings and transaction history, emphasizing clarity and easy scanning. The bill creation interface will feature a multi-column design for item selection and summary. A fixed sidebar or top navigation will ensure quick access to different sections. The design should be responsive to work seamlessly across desktop and tablet devices for countertop use.
- Subtle, non-distracting animations for user feedback, such as smooth transitions when adding items to a bill, confirmation prompts, or subtle hover effects on actionable elements. Animations should enhance usability rather than detract from the functional purpose of the tool.
